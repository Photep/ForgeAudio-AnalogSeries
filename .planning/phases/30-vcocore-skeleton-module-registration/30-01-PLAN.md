<objective>
Clear the one genuinely BLOCKING obstacle in Phase 30, and open the phase's operator surface before anything is committed.

`tests/check_includes.sh` section `[2/7]` ("VCO headers are Rack-free") greps for any include whose path contains `[Rr]ack`.
