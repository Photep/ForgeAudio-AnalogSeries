<objective>
Put the real oscillator inside the Phase-29 seam, and make the Phase 29 → 30 transition impossible to reverse silently.

Phase 29 shipped a boundary contract whose `step()` returns 0 V by design, guarded by a tombstone case that asserts the silence. This plan writes roughly eighteen lines of DSP into that seam — one `exp2_taylor5`, one Nyquist clamp, one double-precision accumulate-and-wrap, one call into the frozen `Waveshape::morphedWave`, one `×5`, three telemetry writes — because four frozen headers already contain everything else. Every line this phase does not write is a line that cannot regress the shipped LFO.

The oscillator is **crude and aliased on purpose**. Phase 32 owns anti-aliasing (CORE-02, AA-01..05); no assertion in this plan or its successors says anything about spectral cleanliness.

The same plan owns the harness's side of the transition, because the two changes are one fact: the tombstone flips from asserting silence to asserting life (D-15), and the two invariants Phase 29 recorded as *green-but-weak* become genuinely load-bearing under real DSP (D-19).

Purpose: make the architecture real end-to-end, with the seam shape unchanged so `VcoBlockDriver` and every Phase-29 assertion keep working untouched.
Output: a live `forge::VcoCore`, and a harness suite that can no longer be satisfied by a stub.
</objective>
