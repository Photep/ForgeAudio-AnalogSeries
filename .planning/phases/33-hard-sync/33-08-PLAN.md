<objective>
Give SC-3 an instrument that can see the artefact it names: a measured per-sample delta bound across resets, paired with a naive-versus-corrected margin that consults no pinned number, plus a discriminating mutation probe — and re-derive the suite's two output tiers for sync rather than assuming them.

Purpose: the alias-floor gate is structurally blind here. Single-sample full-amplitude spikes were measured at zero decibels spectrally, so a sync gate built only on the spectrum would be green on exactly the click SC-3 exists to forbid. And an analytic excursion bound from the waveform's range would be permanently valid and close to vacuous, because a full-scale artefact is what a full-excursion bound admits.

Output: the SC-3 delta bound, its anti-circularity margin, its mutation probe, and the re-derived sync output tier with its provenance, all in `tests/test_vco_core.cpp`.
</objective>
