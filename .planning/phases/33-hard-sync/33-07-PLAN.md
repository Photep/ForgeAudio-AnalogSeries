<objective>
Turn plan 33-05's sync sub-grid from an instrument into a gate: pin per-cell alias-floor thresholds from this phase's own measurements with their provenance, land the snap-to-zero comparison as a permanent case, and re-anchor the placement probe to the seam that now ships.

But the gate must be the one this instrument can honestly carry — the sync correction's own spectral improvement is a fraction of a decibel, so an improvement gate in Phase 32's shape would fail by construction and be loosened until it meant nothing. What the spectral grid CAN evidence is the sub-sample clause of SYNC-02, at a comfortable margin, and a per-cell floor.

Output: pinned thresholds with provenance in `tests/test_vco_spectrum.cpp`, the permanent snap-versus-shipped case, the re-anchored bit-exactness gate, and a mutation probe that fails a stated population exactly.
</objective>
