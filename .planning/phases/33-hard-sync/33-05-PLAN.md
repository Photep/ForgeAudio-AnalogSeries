<objective>
Answer the phase's central question by measurement: where does the sync correction go, given that `MorphBlep`'s seam is future-facing and a Schmitt-detected edge is always in the past?

Purpose: the two-point residual straddles the edge symmetrically, so for a past edge the half that belonged on the already-emitted previous sample is structurally unrecoverable without the output delay buffer Phase 32 rejected — partly citing this phase. Which recoverable half to take, and whether taking the wrong one is worse than taking none, is a question with a measurable answer and a falsifiable in-session prediction. A plan that writes the seam call and measures afterwards has skipped the phase's central question.

Output: the sync sub-grid and its six legs in `tests/test_vco_spectrum.cpp`, a placement probe validated bit-exact against the shipped core, and a RECORDED decision under the three-condition rule that plan 33-06 implements.
</objective>
