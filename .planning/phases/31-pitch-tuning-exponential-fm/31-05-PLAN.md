<objective>
Build the phase's exit gate.

TEST-02 is the criterion this phase is judged on, and the project has twice been bitten by gates
that passed before the thing they measured existed. So this plan builds the gate to be
non-vacuous by construction: the expectation comes from a DIFFERENT implementation than the one
under test, the measurement is taken on the samples a user would hear rather than on the number
`step(...)` just wrote, every grid point expects a distinct frequency, a non-oscillation
precondition runs before any tolerance check, the sweep's limits are derived from the source
constants rather than typed in, and the tolerance is fixed.

The gate also has to stop where the decided behavior stops. The Nyquist clamp intentionally
breaks 1V/oct tracking above its ceiling, and the crossing estimator has its own measured
resolution floor at roughly two samples per cycle. Those are two different limits, and at
48 kHz the estimator's is the tighter one while sitting BELOW the clamp's — so a clamp-only
bound would admit a test point that measures about minus twelve cents on a perfectly correct
oscillator. Both limits are computed and the lesser one binds.

Purpose: an assertion that would fail if the pitch chain were wrong, and that cannot fail
because the apparatus is wrong.
Output: a new test translation unit whose first three invariants are the derived-boundary
self-check and the two tracking tiers.
</objective>
