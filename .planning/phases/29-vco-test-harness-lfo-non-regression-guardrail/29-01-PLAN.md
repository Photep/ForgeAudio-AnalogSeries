<objective>
Create the Phase 29 VCO seam and the Rack-free block-driver harness that drives it — the TEST-01 half of this phase.

Per D-01 this is a **bare POD seam only**: `src/dsp/VcoCore.hpp` establishes the boundary contract (POD `forge::VcoInputs` → `step()` → output voltage + `Telemetry`) mirroring `src/dsp/LfoCore.hpp`, with `step()` returning silence. **No DSP** — all VCO DSP is Phase 30+.

Per D-02, `VcoBlockDriver` exists in Phase 29 to prove the harness *plumbing* (Rack-free, three sample rates, deterministic seam, non-degenerate seeding), deliberately **not** to exercise VCO behavior. This is the conscious opposite of Phase 22's "pull real behavior forward" precedent, because the meaningful behavioral canary for this phase is the shipped-LFO golden replay (plan 29-02), not the not-yet-existent VCO DSP.

**Known coverage caveat (P-7, surfaced deliberately):** while `step()` is silent by design, the "seam determinism" and "output finite" invariants are trivially satisfied and therefore prove nothing about DSP. This plan mitigates that three ways and does not claim coverage it does not have: (a) the invariants are driven with a *varying* input sweep so they become meaningful the instant Phase 30 DSP lands; (b) the `sampleTime` / `sampleRate` injection and the non-degenerate-seed assertions are made genuinely non-vacuous via telemetry and via a real spread-RNG draw; (c) an explicit TOMBSTONE test case asserts the seam is silent by construction, forcing Phase 30 to acknowledge that the seam changed.

Purpose: satisfy ROADMAP success criteria 1 (the VCO harness half) and 2, and give Phases 30–36 a boundary that never has to churn.
Output: `src/dsp/VcoCore.hpp`, `tests/VcoBlockDriver.hpp`, `tests/test_vco_harness.cpp`, all green under `make test`.
</objective>
